Frequently Asked Questions

help_outline How do I apply for a Letter of Credit (LC)?

Navigate to "Trade Finance" in the sidebar navigation menu. Click on "Apply LC", fill out the beneficiary details, specify the payment amount and draft timeline, upload the Proforma Invoice, and submit.

verified How does OCR document scrutiny work?

After the seller uploads the shipping documents (such as eBL, commercial invoice, certificate of origin), our automated OCR service parses the fields. It automatically checks matching values (e.g. quantity, amount, expiry date) against the issued LC terms to alert staff of any discrepancies.

lock How are my collateral funds secured?

During LC approval, the bank places an automated ledger Lien lock on your linked savings account inside the Mifos Fineract core system. These funds remain locked for security and are settled directly to the seller or released upon transaction closure.

swap_horiz What is the typical settlement timeline?

Settlements are processed immediately upon documents auditing. Once the audit desk confirms document validity and clears OCR matches, SWIFT MT756/MT103 instructions are dispatched, and Fineract registers the GL accounting entries in real-time.
