TradeDesk Platform Overview

{{vm.org.description || 'TradeDesk Trade Finance Portal is an enterprise-grade web-self-service client application powered by the Mifos & Fineract core banking engine. It is designed to modernize and digitize trade finance operations, providing a seamless bridge between buyers, sellers, and bank officials.'}}

In a real-world application, this portal operates as a secure transaction portal. When a buyer submits a Letter of Credit (LC) application, the system places an automatic lien on their collateral savings account, processes documents with AI-based OCR readers, generates SWIFT MT700 messages, and automatically posts double-entry GL journal transactions to the core ledger.

Real-Time Lifecycle Workflow

1

Application & Draft

BUYER ROLE

The Buyer enters contract parameters and uploads proforma invoices. The platform verifies funds and registers a pending draft status.

2

Fineract Collateral Lock

BANK STAFF

Upon approval, Fineract Core Banking places an automated ledger Lien on the Buyer's savings account to secure the Letter of Credit.

3

Doc Scrutiny & OCR

SELLER & SYSTEM

The Seller uploads the Electronic Bill of Lading (eBL). The built-in OCR processor instantly checks terms against LC parameters for discrepancies.

4

SWIFT MT700 Settlement

SWIFT NETWORK

Once documents are verified, the Bank generates SWIFT messages, triggers final ledger double-entry settlements, and pays the Seller.
